Governing formulas

Otto cycle equations used with the four-stroke engine simulation.

Symbols
B bore · L stroke · Vc clearance volume · γ specific heat ratio · cv specific heat at constant volume · qin heat added per unit mass · Wnet net work per cylinder per cycle · ncyl number of cylinders · N engine speed (rpm).
The factor 120 = 60 s/min × 2 revolutions per power stroke (four-stroke engine).
Data used in the sample calculations
r = 10 · T1 = 320 K · P1 = 100 kPa · γ = 1.4 · cv = 0.718 kJ/kg·K · cp = 1.005 kJ/kg·K (R = 0.287 kJ/kg·K)
qin = 900 kJ/kg · B = L = 86 mm · ncyl = 4 · N = 2500 rpm
Swept volume
Vs = π4 B2 L
Sample calculation
Vs = (π/4) × (8.6 cm)² × (8.6 cm) = 499.6 cm³
Clearance Vc = Vs/(r − 1) = 499.6/9 = 55.5 cm³
Compression ratio
r = VBDCVTDC = Vs + VcVc
Sample calculation
r = 555.1 / 55.5 = 10.0
Temperature after compression (1→2)
T2 = T1 rγ−1
Sample calculation
T2 = 320 × 100.4 = 803.8 K
Temperature after heat addition (2→3)
T3 = T2 + qincv
Sample calculation
T3 = 803.8 + 900/0.718 = 2057.3 K
Thermal efficiency (air-standard model)
ηth = 1 − 1rγ−1
Sample calculation
ηth = 1 − 1/100.4 = 1 − 0.3981 = 60.2 %
Indicated mean effective pressure
IMEP = WnetVs
Sample calculation
m = P1V1/(R T1) = 6.044×10⁻⁴ kg · wnet = 0.6019 × 900 = 541.7 kJ/kg
Wnet = 0.3274 kJ · IMEP = 0.3274 kJ / 499.6 cm³ = 655.4 kPa
Indicated power
Ẇi = IMEP · Vs · ncyl · N120
Sample calculation
Ẇi = 0.3274 × 4 × 2500/120 = 27.3 kW
